Outs are the river cards that make you the winner. A card that only splits the pot doesn't count, and a dirty out improves you but gives the villain an even better hand. Arcade gives you 45 seconds a hand, or 60 when tapping the cards. An exact answer scores 50 plus up to 250 for the time you have left, so the faster you are, the more it's worth. Exact answers in a row multiply that, up to x3. Off by one scores a quarter of it. Off by two or more costs a chip.
